## About Me

I'm a Computer Science engineer who moved from data analysis into building intelligent systems. My focus is on the layer between "the model works in a notebook" and "the model works in production" — retrieval, evaluation, orchestration, and everything that makes an AI feature reliable rather than a demo.

- 🔭 Currently building **RAG pipelines**, **multi-agent systems**, and **AI-powered products**
- 🌱 Currently deepening **LLM internals**, **agent orchestration (LangGraph)**, and **MLOps**
- 💬 Ask me about **NLP, RAG, LangChain/LangGraph, or ML pipelines**
- 📍 Based in **India** — open to roles in **Pune · Bangalore · Mumbai**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I don't trust "it works" until I've measured *why*
